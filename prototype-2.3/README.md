## Key Rule Changes Incorporated

- Brand 3 affects Launch/Growth products rather than every product.
- Brand 4 and 5 provide stronger mature-brand benefits.
- PPC Specialist savings are limited to twice/year.
- Marketing Manager Organic Social Executive Action is limited to twice/year.
- Email Platform discount is limited to twice/year.
- Christmas PPC Surge is $50K.
- Launch Plan lets a prepared new product buy one launch campaign without consuming MARKET when it first becomes sale-ready; campaign cost is still paid.
- Brand advancement requires three qualifying activities from two categories and at least one customer-facing activity.
- Victory language is consistently $2M annual AFTER-TAX Profit.
