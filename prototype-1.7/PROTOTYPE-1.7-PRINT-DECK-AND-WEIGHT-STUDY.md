## Component Reduction Study — First Pass

The commercial target remains approximately 300 x 300 x 70 mm and preferably below 2.3 kg packed, with a stretch target below 2.0 kg.

### Current high-mass risks
1. Large mounted central board.
2. Four mounted A4 Company Boards.
3. Four punchboards.
4. 144 wooden pieces.
5. 168 final cards.
6. Rigid box and insert.

### Recommended reductions for quotation testing

**Central Board:** investigate reducing final open board from the 840 x 560 mm maximum toward approximately 700 x 500 mm. The current four-A3 paper prototype is intentionally oversized for usability testing.

**Company Boards:** keep 297 x 210 mm during testing, but quote 1.0–1.5 mm board rather than automatically using 2 mm mounted board.

**Action Tiles:** paper prototype tiles are large for readability. Final punchboard target should return to approximately 40 x 30 mm or 40 mm square.

**Player Aids:** prototype is A4. Commercial target should be A5 double-sided if all tables remain readable.

**Wood:** reduce from ~144 toward 100–120 pieces by moving Quality, Price and Life Cycle to punchboard/track markers and using multipurpose player discs.

**Cards:** 168 remains the maximum working commercial count, but after testing target 144–162 if cutting low-value duplicates improves print-sheet efficiency.

**Punchboard:** target 3 sheets rather than 4 if token nesting permits after wooden-piece rationalisation.

## Provisional Mass Budget — Design Target, Not Supplier Quote

Allocate a 2.20 kg internal design ceiling to leave margin below 2.3 kg:
- box lid/base: 350 g
- central mounted board: 500 g
- 4 Company Boards: 260 g
- 168 cards: 300 g
- 3 punchboards/tokens: 270 g
- wooden components: 120 g
- rulebook + player aids: 180 g
- insert/bags/seals: 150 g
- contingency: 70 g

Total target: approximately **2.20 kg**.

These are engineering allowances only. Actual GSM, greyboard density, board area, moisture content, insert construction and manufacturer samples will determine real mass.

## Key Finding

The game can plausibly remain below 2.3 kg, but only if component discipline is maintained. An oversized board + four 2 mm Company Boards + four full punchboards + 144 wood pieces would unnecessarily consume the weight budget.

## Box Fit Direction

A 300 x 300 x 70 mm box remains plausible because:
- folded central board can occupy the full footprint
- A4 Company Boards fit inside 300 mm dimensions
- poker cards can use two deck wells
- reduced wood/punch components can occupy side/central wells
- A5 player aids and a sub-300 mm rulebook nest above components

Final insert engineering must use punched components, not unpunched sheets, as the consumer storage case.

## Manufacturing Decision Rule

Every component must justify itself on four measures:
1. strategic/gameplay value
2. table usability
3. manufacturing cost
4. packed mass/volume

A visually impressive component that adds no meaningful decision should be removed or simplified.
