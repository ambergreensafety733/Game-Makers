## Setup by Player Count

### 2 Players
Use 12 random Concepts, 8 Forecasts, all 6 Factories, 4 Licences, 4 Retail cards. Add Neutral Competitors.

### 3 Players
Use all 16 Concepts, all Forecasts, all Factories, 5 Licences, 5 Retail cards.

### 4 Players
Use all 16 Concepts, all Forecasts, all Factories, all Licences and Retail cards.

Always display only 5 Concepts, 3 Licences and 3 Retail/Opportunity cards at one time.

## Playtest Rule
Do not add new card types during a test simply because an interesting idea occurs. Record the idea and finish the game under the rules that began the test. This protects the usefulness of the test data.
