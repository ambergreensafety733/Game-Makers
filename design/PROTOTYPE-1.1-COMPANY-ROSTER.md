# GAME MAKERS: The $2 Million Race
## Prototype 1.1 — Ten Player Companies

The companies give players strategic identity without deciding the game before it begins.

## Design Rule

Beginner Mode: company abilities are ignored; players choose by name/artwork only.

Advanced Mode: each company receives one modest permanent advantage and one strategic identity. Powers must save or create roughly comparable value over a normal game and must not remove core decisions.

# 1. Dragon Forge Games

**Identity:** Premium tabletop specialist
**Visual:** dragon wrapped around a metallic game box; deep crimson/gold direction.
**Tagline:** BIG WORLDS. BOLD GAMES.

### Advanced Ability — Premium Craft
The first Quality upgrade purchased for a Standard or Deluxe game costs $10K less. Premium price penalty is reduced by 1 on one Quality 5 product at a time.

### Natural Strategy
Higher-quality Standard/Deluxe games, awards and premium pricing.

### Trade-off
Still carries the high development, stock and freight exposure of large games.

# 2. Tiny Giant Games

**Identity:** Small boxes, large sales
**Visual:** tiny meeple casting a giant shadow; bright cyan/orange direction.
**Tagline:** SMALL BOX. BIG IDEA.

### Advanced Ability — Compact Efficiency
Card and Compact games use one less warehouse space per 2K stock, minimum normal handling requirement. Once per year, one 5K+ Card/Compact production run receives an additional $10K production saving.

### Natural Strategy
Card games, Compact games, efficient inventory and rapid portfolio growth.

### Trade-off
No benefit on Standard/Deluxe products.

# 3. Storyforge Publishing

**Identity:** Books, stories and language publishing
**Visual:** open book becoming a fantasy landscape; emerald/cream direction.
**Tagline:** STORIES WITHOUT BORDERS.

### Advanced Ability — Publishing Network
Book localisation costs $10K less. The first 1K KDP sales each year generates an additional $3K income.

### Natural Strategy
Game books, KDP, direct physical books and translations.

### Trade-off
Limited advantage for large physical board games.

# 4. Direct Hit Games

**Identity:** Direct-to-consumer specialist
**Visual:** parcel/target/storefront motif; vivid lime/charcoal direction.
**Tagline:** OWN THE CUSTOMER.

### Advanced Ability — Direct Community
Website Level 1 costs $10K less to open. Website capacity is +1K per quarter for one chosen product.

### Natural Strategy
Website, community building, higher-margin direct sales.

### Trade-off
Must still generate sufficient Appeal/demand; capacity does not guarantee customers.

# 5. Market Rocket Games

**Identity:** Amazon and high-volume marketplace specialist
**Visual:** rocket formed from parcels/meeples; electric blue/red direction.
**Tagline:** LAUNCH. SCALE. SELL.

### Advanced Ability — Marketplace Optimisation
One Amazon Ads action per year costs $10K less. Once per quarter, one Amazon product with Appeal 11+ may increase its Sales Claim by 1K if market demand and stock remain.

### Natural Strategy
Amazon volume, Bestseller pursuit and fast scaling.

### Trade-off
Amazon remains a lower-margin channel than a developed Website.

# 6. Global Meeple Licensing

**Identity:** International rights and licensing specialist
**Visual:** globe surrounded by game pieces and contract seal; violet/turquoise direction.
**Tagline:** ONE GAME. EVERY MARKET.

### Advanced Ability — Deal Makers
When claiming a Licence Offer, treat Reputation as +1 for eligibility/tie-breaking. Each accepted licence advance gains +$10K.

### Natural Strategy
Licensing, international rights and low-inventory expansion.

### Trade-off
Self-publishing can produce greater long-run margin in successful markets.

# 7. EuroPlay Studios

**Identity:** Fast, flexible European production
**Visual:** stylised European factory with board-game pieces; cobalt/yellow direction.
**Tagline:** CLOSER. FASTER. FLEXIBLE.

### Advanced Ability — Nearshore Network
Once per quarter, reduce one European manufacturing/freight order by $10K, provided the order is at least 3K units.

### Natural Strategy
Lower lead-time exposure and responsive stock replenishment.

### Trade-off
China may still offer better economics for very large planned runs.

# 8. BuzzWorks Games

**Identity:** Marketing and audience-building specialist
**Visual:** speech bubble/megaphone exploding into colourful pieces; magenta/black direction.
**Tagline:** MAKE THEM TALK.

### Advanced Ability — Campaign Engine
The first Marketing action each year costs $10K less. Once per year, a product receiving at least +2 paid Marketing also receives +1 extra Appeal that quarter.

### Natural Strategy
Launches, social campaigns, direct communities and trend exploitation.

### Trade-off
Marketing still consumes actions and money and cannot rescue a fundamentally poor stock/price decision.

# 9. Inventor's Table Games

**Identity:** Product design and development specialist
**Visual:** drafting table, pencil, prototype pieces and lightbulb; amber/navy direction.
**Tagline:** BUILD THE NEXT HIT.

### Advanced Ability — Prototype Culture
One product development upgrade per year costs $20K less. Custom Concepts have Base Appeal 4 instead of 3.

### Natural Strategy
Custom concepts, Quality and adapting portfolio to gaps competitors leave open.

### Trade-off
Strong products still require manufacturing, marketing and channel execution.

# 10. Steady Hand Publishing

**Identity:** Finance, cash flow and controlled growth
**Visual:** balanced scales formed from coins and game boxes; forest green/silver direction.
**Tagline:** PROFIT BY DESIGN.

### Advanced Ability — Financial Discipline
Standard loan annual interest is $20K instead of $30K for this company. At Annual Accounts, reduce base overhead by $10K.

### Natural Strategy
Cash management, measured expansion and efficient capital use.

### Trade-off
Does not directly increase product Appeal or demand.

# Company Selection

## Beginner
Choose any company. Ignore Advanced Ability.

## Advanced — Open Selection
Lay all 10 Company cards face-up. Randomise/rotate selection order, then choose.

## Advanced — Strategic Selection
Reveal Year 1 Market Outlook before company selection. Lowest/last initiative choice method to be tested so first pick is not excessively valuable.

# Balance Guardrails

A company power must not:
- provide automatic sales without demand
- ignore stock requirements
- eliminate factory lead times entirely
- provide immunity to market competition
- create a private random advantage
- make one sales channel mandatory
- exceed approximately $50K–$100K expected annual equivalent benefit without a meaningful condition

Powers should encourage strategies, not dictate them.

# Company Card Specification

Working card: oversized identity card or standard 63 x 88 mm if legible.

Front:
- company name
- logo
- tagline
- identity
- Advanced Ability
- player-colour compatibility symbol

Back:
- common GAME MAKERS branding

The Company Board remains universal rather than printing ten different large boards. This reduces manufacturing cost, box weight and balancing problems. The chosen Company card slots into the universal board.

# Visual Identity Requirement

The ten companies must look like believable fictional international games publishers, not fantasy factions. Artwork should combine business/publishing cues with board-game energy.

They should be clearly distinguishable in marketing images and on the table.
