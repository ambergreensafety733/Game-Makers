## Event Design Rule
Events should create opportunities as often as problems. Players should usually be able to react rather than simply suffer unavoidable losses.

## Licence Choice
Accepting a licence can generate immediate cash and low-risk recurring income. Rejecting it preserves the potentially larger profit from self-publishing. This should be one of the game's clearest strategic choices.

# F. BESTSELLER & AWARD TOKENS

## Bestseller
A product that sells at least 8K copies in one quarter, or qualifies through an Event, gains a **BESTSELLER** token.

Working effect:
- +1 Base Demand while in Growth/Mature stage.
- First Bestseller earned by a company also gives +1 Reputation.

## Award
Quality 4–5 products can compete for Awards through Events/Campaigns.

Working effect:
- +1 permanent Base Demand.
- +1 Company Reputation.
- Improves premium licensing eligibility.

A product can normally hold only one Bestseller and one Award token.

# G. PLAYER INTERACTION PRINCIPLES

GAME MAKERS should create competition without relying on aggressive attack cards.

Players interact through:
- Limited factory capacity.
- Competing products reducing available market demand.
- Limited Retail Contracts.
- Limited Licence Offers.
- Market trends that reward timing.
- Awards and conventions.
- Price competition.
- Competition for Bestseller status.

A player's successful decision can make another player's plan less attractive, but players should rarely be able to simply destroy another player's company.
