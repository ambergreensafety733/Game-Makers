# GAME MAKERS: The $2 Million Race
## Prototype 1.2 — Growth Curve, Market Scaling & Provisional BOM

This version establishes the economic destination of the game and converts player-count scaling into printed values. Figures remain balance-test values until physical playtesting.

# 1. The $2 Million Race Must Feel Earned

Starting Cash: $500K.
Victory: first company to record $2M annual after-tax profit.
Working maximum: end of Year 6.

The intended game arc is not linear. Players begin as small publishers and progressively build products, reputation, channels, territories and licensing income.

A $2M winner should normally require several successful products and/or a strong international/licensing engine rather than one lucky hit.

# 2. Target Annual Profit Curve for a Strong Company

These are design targets, not guaranteed income:

- Year 1: −$100K to +$100K
- Year 2: $100K–$300K
- Year 3: $300K–$650K
- Year 4: $650K–$1.1M
- Year 5: $1.1M–$1.7M
- Year 6: $1.6M–$2.3M+

Exceptional play can reach $2M during Year 5. Ordinary competent play should usually need Year 6. Poor strategic decisions can leave a company far below the target.

# 3. How Profit Scales

The route to $2M should come from a portfolio rather than inflation of one sales number.

Example mature winning business annual gross-profit engine:

- 2 established Amazon products: approximately $300K–$450K
- 2 developed direct Website products: approximately $350K–$500K
- 1 strong USA/EU product: approximately $250K–$400K
- KDP/physical books: approximately $100K–$200K
- licensing royalties/advances: approximately $250K–$450K
- retail/seasonal opportunities: approximately $150K–$300K

Potential gross contribution/profit engine before company overhead/marketing/tax: roughly $1.4M–$2.3M. A true $2M after-tax winner therefore needs especially strong execution, a larger portfolio, premium margins, major licences or late-game scale improvements.

# 4. Late-Game Scale Mechanic

To make $2M reachable without simply multiplying all market demand, introduce COMPANY SCALE benefits earned by building a real business.

## Startup
Starting level. No scale benefit.

## Growing
Requirement: $500K+ annual revenue and 2 active products.
Benefit: one extra Business Action per YEAR, used in a different category from the normal action.

## Established
Requirement: $1.5M+ annual revenue and Reputation 4+.
Benefit: once per quarter choose one: +1K Website capacity OR reduce one manufacturing order by $10K.

## International
Requirement: active in 2+ territories and $3M+ annual revenue.
Benefit: one additional secondary Business Action in Q2 and Q4 only.

## Global
Requirement: active in UK/EU/USA, Reputation 7+, $5M+ annual revenue.
Benefit: +1K Sales Claim on one product per quarter where demand/stock exist.

Scale benefits create operational capability but do not create customers automatically.

# 5. Action Economy Protection

Extra scale actions are limited because unrestricted actions would create runaway leaders.

Secondary actions cannot repeat the player's main action category that quarter unless a specific ability permits it.

Company Scale is public, allowing rivals to anticipate capability.

# 7. EU and USA Demand

To avoid three enormous duplicated market tables, EU/USA unlock through compact territory overlays.

Working demand multiplier encoded directly on overlay tracks:
- EU ≈ UK 3-player standard values when open.
- USA ≈ UK values +25–40% depending genre, rounded to printed whole units.

Final version will print exact numbers; players will never calculate percentages.

# 8. Company Ability Balance Test — Initial

Target annual equivalent value of most abilities: approximately $20K–$60K early game, potentially $60K–$100K when deliberately exploited late game.

Initial assessment:
- Dragon Forge: conditional, medium value — retain.
- Tiny Giant: low/medium direct saving plus inventory efficiency — retain for testing.
- Storyforge: modest KDP/localisation advantage — may need slight strengthening after book tests.
- Direct Hit: strong if Website-focused but capacity still needs demand — retain.
- Market Rocket: potentially strong at high Appeal — cap extra Sales Claim to once/quarter retained.
- Global Meeple: advance bonus predictable; Reputation tie benefit situational — retain.
- EuroPlay: predictable $10K/quarter maximum = $40K/year — balanced baseline.
- BuzzWorks: approximately $10K saving plus one +1 bonus — possibly low; test.
- Inventor's Table: potentially $20K/year plus Custom Concept flexibility — strategic rather than cash-heavy.
- Steady Hand: up to $20K loan-interest saving plus $10K overhead = modest defensive power.

No ability currently grants random outcomes.

# 10. Inventory Cash Rule

When ordering 8K China Standard at the 5K–9K band:
- Cost = 8 × $18K = $144K Cash immediately.
- Eight stock cubes eventually arrive.
- Profit is unaffected until cubes sell.

If 5 cubes sell at $29K Amazon contribution:
- Cash increases $145K.
- Profit increases 5 × ($29K − $18K) = $55K.
- Three cubes remain inventory.

The player should be able to perform this from printed tables without a calculator; common differences will be printed as gross-profit values on aids.

# 16. Manufacturing Weight Warning

168 cards + four mounted Company Boards + a large folding board + four punchboards + ~144 wooden pieces may push the game toward the upper end of the 2.0–2.3 kg target.

Before artwork lock, perform a component mass estimate and investigate:
- reducing central board area
- using thinner Company Boards if usability remains good
- converting some wood to punchboard
- reducing unnecessary token duplication
- optimising card count to printer sheets

Do not increase the retail box merely to accommodate avoidable component excess.
