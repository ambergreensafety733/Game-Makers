# GAME MAKERS: The $2 Million Race
## Prototype 1.5 — Printable Prototype Build

Prototype 1.5 is the first version where the design moves from written specifications to real-size printable physical components.

Created/tested locally at 100% scale:
- Central Board v1 — four A3 landscape sheets
- Universal Company Board v1 — A4 landscape
- Action Tiles v1 — six cut-out tiles
- Player Aid v1 — double-sided A4 prototype
- Sample Cards v1 — 63 x 88 mm
- Playtest Tracking Sheet v1 — A4 landscape

The first board and component PDFs have been render-checked for clipping and basic layout integrity.

## Physical Test Purpose

This is deliberately low-art. The aim is to discover:
- whether the board is too large
- whether Company Board information is readable
- whether 63 x 88 mm cards hold enough information
- whether stock cubes and markers can be handled comfortably
- whether sales/accounting can be resolved from the Player Aid
- whether two Business Rounds per quarter produce good pacing
- whether the game can remain within the intended 2–3 hour Standard Game target

## Print Instructions

Print PDFs at **Actual Size / 100%**. Do not use Fit to Page.

Central Board: print all four A3 landscape sheets and arrange as a 2 x 2 prototype board.
Company Board: print four copies.
Action Tiles: print four copies, preferably on card or mount to thicker card.
Player Aid: print four copies; duplex if desired.
Cards: print on card or paper and sleeve with spare playing cards for stiffness.
Tracking Sheet: print one per playtest or one per player if detailed data is wanted.

## Temporary Components

Until manufactured prototype pieces exist, use generic cubes/counters for stock and tracking. 1 Stock Cube = 1,000 copies.

## Artwork Status

NOT FINAL ARTWORK.

No final illustration, branding polish, production bleed/dielines or manufacturer colour profiles should be locked until physical testing validates the information architecture and dimensions.

## Manufacturing Feedback

Prototype 1.5 gives the first measured dimensions that can now be fed back into the living Manufacturing & Print Specification.

Key measured prototype dimensions:
- Main board: 4 x A3 sheets for table test; final mounted board engineering still TBD
- Company Board: 297 x 210 mm
- Main cards: 63 x 88 mm
- Action tile target: approximately 80 x 48 mm in the first paper test; expected to reduce materially for final punchboard
- Player Aid: A4 prototype; likely reduce after usability test

These measurements should be treated as usability tests, not final factory dimensions.
