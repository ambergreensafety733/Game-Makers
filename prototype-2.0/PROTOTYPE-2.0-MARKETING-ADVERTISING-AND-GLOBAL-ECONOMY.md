# GAME MAKERS: The $2 Million Race
## Prototype 2.0 — Marketing, Advertising & Global Economy Lock Pass 1

Marketing and advertising are now elevated from a small Appeal modifier to a core business system. A successful publisher should have to decide not only what to make, where to manufacture and where to sell, but how much to spend creating demand and building a brand.

The design remains decision-first: advertising has known core effects. Players choose audience, timing, product, territory and budget rather than rolling to see whether an advert works.

# 1. Marketing Budget

MARKET is one of the six core Business Actions.

When taking MARKET, choose one campaign. Unless stated otherwise, the effect lasts for the current quarter.

## Campaign Menu

| Campaign | Cost | Core effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Organic Social / Community | $10K | +1 Appeal to one product; also counts toward Brand Building |
| Amazon Sponsored Ads | $20K | +2 Appeal to one product on Amazon in one territory |
| Influencer / Reviewer Campaign | $20K | +2 Appeal to one product; Quality 4+ gains +1 Reputation if it sells 3K+ that quarter |
| Email / Customer Campaign | $20K | +2 Website Appeal to up to two existing products |
| Trade / Retail Marketing | $30K | +2 Retail Deal score and +1 Appeal for qualifying retail sales |
| Major Digital Campaign | $40K | +3 Appeal to one product across Amazon + Website in one territory |
| Launch Campaign | $50K | +4 Appeal to one product in its Launch quarter only |
| International Campaign | $60K | +3 Appeal to one product in two opened territories |

No campaign creates sales beyond available demand, stock or channel capacity.

# 2. Advertising Saturation

Spending more is not always better.

A single product may normally receive no more than +5 temporary Appeal from paid campaigns in one quarter.

A second paid campaign on the same product in the same quarter costs an additional $10K Agency/Creative fee.

A third paid campaign on the same product is not permitted without a specific Company/Staff ability.

This stops advertising becoming a simple money-to-victory conversion.

# 3. Brand Building

Companies can invest in the publisher brand rather than only one product.

Track **Brand Strength 0–5** on the Company Board.

Start: Brand 0.

At Annual Accounts, gain +1 Brand if during that year the company completed at least THREE qualifying brand-building activities, from at least TWO different categories:
- Organic Social / Community campaign
- Influencer/Reviewer campaign on a product that sold 3K+
- Award win
- Bestseller achievement
- successful Retail Contract
- successful Licence Deal
- 4K+ Website sales in one quarter

Maximum +1 Brand per year normally.

Brand effect:
- Brand 0: no effect
- Brand 1: no numeric effect; recognised publisher
- Brand 2: +1 Appeal to Launch products
- Brand 3: +1 Appeal to all products
- Brand 4: +1 Appeal to all products and +1 Deal score
- Brand 5: +2 Appeal to Launch products, +1 to other products, +1 Deal score

Brand rewards sustained marketing/business success without requiring permanent advertising bookkeeping every quarter.

# 4. Marketing Staff / Agencies

Add Staff/Service cards such as:

**Freelance Designer — $20K/year**
First Launch Campaign each year costs $10K less.

**PPC Specialist — $30K/year**
Amazon Sponsored Ads cost $10K less, maximum once per quarter.

**PR Agency — $40K/year**
Influencer/Reviewer campaigns gain +1 Deal/Award score where applicable.

**Marketing Manager — $50K/year**
Once per quarter, one $10K Organic Social campaign may be performed as an Executive Action.

**International Marketing Agency — $60K/year**
International Campaign costs $10K less and may cover three opened territories at Global Scale.

These create the real business decision between outsourcing, hiring capability and paying campaign-by-campaign.

# 5. Marketing Forecasts

Marketing conditions become part of Forecast planning.

Examples:
- AMAZON CPC SURGE: Sponsored Ads cost +$10K next quarter.
- VIRAL BOARD-GAME TREND: Influencer campaigns gain +1 Appeal next quarter.
- CHRISTMAS AD CLUTTER: Q4 paid campaigns cost +$10K but physical demand is high.
- DIRECT CUSTOMER BOOM: Email/Website campaigns gain +1 Appeal.
- REVIEWER SEASON: Quality 4+ launches gain +1 from Influencer campaigns.
- AD PLATFORM CHANGE: Major Digital Campaign costs +$10K.

Players normally receive warning through NEXT/LATER SIGNAL before exact effects apply.

# 6. Marketing vs Price

This is now a central strategic tension.

A company can:
- lower price to increase Appeal
- spend on advertising to increase Appeal
- invest permanently in Quality
- build Reputation/Brand
- combine some of these

Budget price gives +2 Appeal but reduces contribution.
A $20K advertising campaign can give +2 while preserving Standard/Premium margin.

The better choice depends on expected unit sales, margin and competitors.

# 7. Marketing ROI Is Not Automatic

Example:

A Standard game earns $11K Profit contribution per 1K at its current channel/Cost of Sales.

A $40K campaign that creates only 2K additional sales has generated roughly $22K additional gross profit and was commercially poor.

If the same campaign produces 5K additional sales or helps win a retailer/award, it may be excellent.

The player should therefore think like a real publisher: **will this campaign pay for itself?**

# 8. Competitive Advertising

Campaign selections remain hidden until Action reveal.

If multiple publishers advertise heavily into the same genre/territory, normal product Saturation still applies.

Players can therefore create an expensive marketing battle that benefits neither as much as moving into a less contested market.

# 9. Product Launch Window

New products receive a distinct Launch quarter.

Launch Campaign is only available in that quarter.

If a player launches with insufficient stock, the advertising spend may be wasted because Sales remain stock-limited.

If a player manufactures too much before validating demand, inventory risk increases.

This creates a deliberate launch-planning puzzle across DESIGN, PRODUCE and MARKET.

# 10. Marketing Research

MARKET fallback remains:

Pay $10K for Market Research and choose ONE:
- inspect the exact effect behind the next hidden Forecast signal, OR
- inspect the next two Concept cards and return them in either order, OR
- inspect one opponent's face-down Price marker before setting your own price this quarter (once per opponent per year).

The third option adds competitive intelligence while remaining deterministic.

# 13. International Channel Economics

To keep play manageable, EU and USA use the same base contribution tables as UK plus a printed territory modifier rather than separate giant tables.

EU:
- Amazon contribution: UK value +$1K per 1K sold
- Website contribution: UK value +$2K per 1K sold

USA:
- Amazon contribution: UK value +$3K per 1K sold
- Website contribution: UK value +$4K per 1K sold

These are game abstractions covering market pricing/channel economics, not tax/accounting claims.

USA sales do not use a UK-style VAT deduction mechanic in the game; territory economics are already abstracted into contribution values.

# 14. VAT and Tax Simplification

VAT/sales-tax complexity must be visible conceptually without turning the game into an accounting exercise.

Channel contribution values are treated as **net publisher contribution after ordinary marketplace/channel/VAT-type transaction effects**.

Players do not calculate VAT sale by sale.

Corporation tax remains an Annual Accounts decision/result using the simplified game tax table.

This preserves the commercial lesson: gross selling price is not the money the publisher keeps.

# 18. Balance Effect

Marketing is now a serious alternative use of Cash and Actions.

A company that manufactures an excellent game but never markets it may lose to a slightly weaker product with better positioning and advertising.

A company that spends excessively on advertising can destroy its own Profit.

A strong player should learn when to:
- advertise
- reduce price
- improve Quality
- build Brand
- target direct customers
- target Amazon
- pursue retail publicity
- stop spending because the market is already saturated
