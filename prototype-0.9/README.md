# GAME MAKERS: The $2 Million Race
## Prototype 0.9 — First Printable Table Prototype

This folder defines the first low-art version intended to be printed, cut out and physically playtested before final illustration and manufacturing artwork.

## Prototype objective

Test the game, not the artwork. Every component should make business decisions easy to understand and should minimise bookkeeping.

## Printable package

### Sheet 1–4 — Central Board
Print as four A3 landscape sheets and join to create an approximately A1 playing area.

Board zones:
1. GAME MAKERS title and $2M victory track.

Each player receives:
- DESIGN
- PRODUCE

- EXPAND

Action tiles are selected secretly and revealed simultaneously.

## Player Aid — Side A

### Quarter Sequence
1. Advance forecast and year marker.
2. Update market conditions.
3. Secretly choose Action tiles.
4. Reveal actions.
5. Resolve business actions.
6. Allocate stock.
7. Resolve market sales by Appeal.
8. Update Cash and Profit.
9. Advance product/shipping markers.

### Appeal
Base Appeal + Quality + Reputation + Trend + Price + Marketing + Channel + Awards/Bestseller − Saturation.

### Sales Claim
- Appeal 0–1 = 1K
- Appeal 2–4 = 2K
- Appeal 5–7 = 3K
- Appeal 8–10 = 4K
- Appeal 11–13 = 5K
- Appeal 14+ = 6K

## First Playtest Setup

For the first physical test use only:
- 4 companies
- UK market
- Amazon + Website
- China + Europe manufacturing
- 12 Concept cards
- 8 Forecast cards
- 6 Licence cards
- 6 Retail/Opportunity cards

Do not initially use USA, advanced company powers or every staff card. Add them after the core loop is proven enjoyable.

## First Playtest Questions

After every quarter record:
1. Did every player understand why their sales result occurred?
2. Was any decision obvious rather than interesting?
3. Did anyone need a calculator?
4. Did players pay attention to competitors?
5. Was there too much administration?
6. Did stock quantity feel important?
7. Did price matter enough?
8. Was Quality worth investing in?
9. Did Website versus Amazon create a genuine choice?
10. Was the Forecast useful for planning?

After the game record:
- winner
- finishing annual profits
- cash positions
- product counts
- unsold stock
- dominant channels
- game duration
- number of rulebook lookups
- most enjoyable decision
- least enjoyable mechanism

## Visual Direction for Prototype

Low-art but colourful and immediately readable. Use large icons and genre colour bands. Never rely on colour alone: every genre also gets a unique icon and written label.

The eventual finished product should look like a modern international strategy game, but final artwork should not be locked until playtesting proves the mechanics.
